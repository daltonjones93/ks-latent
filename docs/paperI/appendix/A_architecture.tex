\para{Selection of the $\alpha=0.1$ model.} Six variants were trained at
$\alpha=0.1$ (Sec.~\ref{sec:fail_mse}). The main-text model was chosen by a
rule that uses no Lyapunov truth; the rule was written after the variants
had been scored against the truths then available, but before its own
metric was computed (Sec.~\ref{sec:canonical_results}). (i)
Eligibility: free rollouts of 1000 steps from two initial conditions on
the $1\times$, $2\times$ and $3\times$ boxes, statistics over steps
100--1000; the vorticity standard deviation and the kinetic-energy share
in $|\bm k|\in[3,5]$, averaged over the two initial conditions, must lie
within $15\%$ of their values in the training data (1.528 and 0.599) at
every size. Large-scale statistics are excluded because they depend on the
domain size. (ii) Selection: the lowest normalized forecast error
$\langle\|\hat u-u\|^2\rangle/\Var(u)$ at lead $k=8$ (1.6 time units)
on the held-out validation trajectory, with the model deployed on the
full $32\pi\times2\pi$ domain and started from 19 snapshots. Variants
within $2\%$ would be separated by the smaller deviation in (i). Results
for all six variants are in \texttt{artifacts/experiments/a10\_selection/}.

\para{The latent coherence penalty of the 1D model.} The 1D model was
trained with an additional Stage~A term of weight $0.01$ that rewards
positive correlation between latent coordinates that are close in the
site-major latent index, that is, mostly the channels of the same and
neighbouring sites. Over a batch it computes the matrix $C$ of signed Pearson
correlations between the $\dlat$ latent coordinates and returns
$-\big(\sum_{i\neq j} W_{ij}C_{ij}/\sum_{i\neq j}W_{ij} - \overline{C}_{\rm off}\big)$,
where $W_{ij}=\exp(-d_{ij}^2/18)$ is a Gaussian weight in the circular
distance $d_{ij}$ between indices and $\overline{C}_{\rm off}$ the plain mean of the
off-diagonal correlations; ``signed'' means that anti-correlated
neighbours are penalized rather than rewarded. The 2D models do not use
it, and we have not measured the 1D model's sensitivity to it.
