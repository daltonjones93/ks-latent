%======================================================================
\section{A second system: Kolmogorov flow}
\label{sec:kolmogorov_da}
%======================================================================

Kolmogorov flow with linear drag (Paper~I, Sec.~4) tests the filter on chaos that is organized by a
forcing rather than isotropic. We use the drag $\alpha=0.05$, a channel of height $2\pi$, and a
per-patch model with $c=12$ channels trained, as the 2D KS model, on noisy strips (noise $0.1$ of the
field's standard deviation) of one wide domain and applied at widths $2\pi$ ($1\times$, $8\times8$ sites)
and $6\pi$ ($3\times$). Its Kaplan--Yorke dimension from one initial condition is $22.4$, $47.9$ and
$69.8$ at $1\times$, $2\times$ and $3\times$, against truth means of $21.5$ and $48.6$ and a single truth
run of $75.2$ (Paper~I, Table~III): $+4\%$, $-1\%$ and $-7\%$. Vorticity is observed every eighth grid
point in $x$ and $y$ with error $0.1$ of the standard deviation ($0.141$), every time unit, about $0.08$
Lyapunov times as for 2D KS. The filter is that of Sec.~\ref{sec:tilings_method} with the same settings,
64 members and 100 cycles, not retuned; errors are steady-state, in vorticity units.

\para{Tilings in $x$ only.} The flow is invariant to shifts in $x$, but in $y$ only by a whole forcing
period, 32 grid points. Half-patch shifts in $y$, a quarter period, show the encoder patches at forcing
phases it never saw, and in a first test with the 6-channel model of Paper I those tilings failed
(errors of $0.66$, against $0.17$ for four tilings shifted in $x$) although their decoders were only
modestly worse. The four
tilings are therefore shifted by $0$, $4$, $8$ and $12$ grid points in $x$ alone. This is the general rule
behind the 2D KS construction: tilings may be shifted only along the symmetries the model was trained on.

\para{Results.} At $1\times$ the latent filter's error is $0.115$ ($0.105$--$0.124$, seeds 0--2), $43\%$
below interpolation of the observations ($0.202$) and below the observation error; the free run's is
$1.13$, and one tiling gives $0.147$. At $3\times$ (seed 0) it is $0.113$ against $0.201$ for
interpolation, and $0.137$ with one tiling, so the error does not grow with the width. RTPS $0.9$ or
model noise $0.04$ change it by less than $0.002$ (seed 0). The decoder floor, the error of encoding and
decoding the truth, is $0.078$ for one tiling and $0.059$ blended, half the analysis error. The ensemble
is under-dispersed, with spread over error $0.52$--$0.58$ with four tilings and $0.70$--$0.73$ with one,
against $0.92$--$0.99$ with four tilings in 2D KS (Sec.~\ref{sec:da_tilings}); we did not retune the
inflation for it.
We did not run a physics-based comparator for this system.
