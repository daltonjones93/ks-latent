Research Update

Localized Latent Fields for Data Assimilation

A spatial, translation-equivariant latent representation for chaotic PDEs — locality, interpretability, and zero-retraining domain transfer, tested end to end on Kuramoto–Sivashinsky.
KS · Lorenz-96 · a path toward Rayleigh–Bénard and NCAR’s CM1
Motivation

Reduced-order models for chaotic PDEs are usually a flat vector — no structure, no locality, no transfer.

1

Cheap surrogates exist — a small autoencoder + propagator can stand in for an expensive solver.

2

But a flat vector latent has no physical distance — you can’t localize an ensemble filter on it, and it can’t change size with the domain.

3

A genuinely spatial latent — a field, not a vector — might fix both at once, for free.

Architecture

A genuine spatial encoder — not a reshaped vector.

1 · Patchify
Non-overlapping Conv1d, stride = kernel. Each of n_sites patches gets its own embedding.
→
2 · Circular site-mixing
Stacked circular Conv1d layers let neighboring sites exchange information — bounded, exact receptive field.
→
3 · Anchor + residual
Channel 0 is never learned — fixed to the site’s own physical average. Remaining channels are free, learned hidden state.
Site-major

The raw latent index is physical position — no learned reordering needed for locality diagnostics to mean something.

Exact equivariance

Shift the input by one patch → the whole latent field shifts by one site, provably, by construction.

Extensive by design

d_latent = n_sites × local_channels — grows with the domain, the way the true attractor dimension does.

Training recipe

Three lessons, one clean result.

Barrier > hinge

A log-barrier growth penalty repels approach to a blow-up threshold; a squared hinge only reacts after crossing it.

Always run Stage 2

Stage-1-only chaos checks are unreliable. Only a standalone, 2000-step Stage-2 rollout is trustworthy.

Smaller latent won

d=48 beat d=96 on every metric — less redundant budget forced a cleaner representation.

Section 224, free-running rollout — error climbs then saturates at √2, the signature of genuine chaos, not divergence or collapse.

Alternate candidate 1

Spectral-gap criterion — does a system’s spectral gap quantitatively predict how much propagator memory a small latent needs? One suggestive data point, not yet a validated law.

Alternate candidate 2

Stage-2 chaos-collapse critique — multi-step MSE training destroys measured chaos while improving forecast skill, across systems and architectures. A validated problem; no validated fix yet.

A promising side-result

Interpretable local closures — real structure, not a handwritten equation.

Stencil regression

A shared, local, quadratic closure law explains 90% of 224’s dynamics at full width, vs 82% for a less-local checkpoint — real, measurable structure.

Sparse selection (SINDy)

Doesn’t collapse to a handwritten equation — but a genuine ~5× compression exists (252→49 terms), and survivors are mechanistically informative: the physical channel needs the learned hidden channels to predict itself.

What it actually takes

Validate RBC’s own chaos → a genuine 2D local field (circular horizontal, bounded vertical) → repeat this whole arc. Not a rerun — new architecture, new validation.

Summary

A real mechanism, not yet a paper.

Solid
  • Architecture + training recipe that reliably produces bounded, genuinely chaotic local latent fields.
  • Zero-retraining domain transfer — novel, cleanly demonstrated at 2× and 4×.
  • Localization becomes necessary, not just helpful, as the domain grows past the trained size.
Not yet
  • The headline DA claim — local + Gaspari-Cohn beats SEC — is a clean negative at the trained domain size.
  • The L=200 result is one data point, not a swept, defended result.
  • Everything here is KS only — L96 already shows the recipe doesn’t transfer for free.
