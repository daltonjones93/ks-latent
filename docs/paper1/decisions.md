## 2026-10-08 — A2: float32 vs float64 tangent propagation

**Code.** `lyapunov_spectrum_latent_propagator(..., tangent_dtype=torch.float64)` runs a float64
copy of the propagator (single_state mode; tests in `tests/unit/test_lyapunov_latent.py`).
Runner: `scripts/lyap_dtype_control.py`. Consolidated:
`artifacts/experiments/lyap_dtype_control.json`.

**Result (three ICs per system; QR is float64 in both).**

| system | float32 mean | float64 mean | Δ mean | IC range (float32) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1D KS, L = 100 | 22.24 | 22.24 | +0.00% | 1.0% |
| 1D KS, L = 200 | 44.64 | 44.62 | −0.06% | 0.7% |
| 2D KS, 44² | 83.51 | 83.76 | +0.30% | 1.0% |
| Kolmogorov α = 0.05, 1× | 23.26 | 22.75 | −2.2% | 25% |

**Deviation from the pre-registered rule, stated.** The rule ("re-run everything in float64
if D_KY changes > 0.5%") compared single runs. A round-off-level change sends a chaotic
trajectory onto a different finite-time path, so a single-run comparison measures sampling
noise, not precision: two float64 runs of the identical 1D configuration in different
processes gave 21.78 and 22.18. The correct test is the difference of IC-averaged means
against the IC spread. By that test there is no precision bias in any system, and model
spectra are **not** re-run in float64.

**Text for §3.6:** "Tangent vectors are propagated in single precision and re-orthonormalized
by a double-precision QR; a double-precision control changes the three-IC mean D_KY by at
most 0.3% (1D and 2D KS) and 2.2% (Kolmogorov flow at native size, against a 25% spread across
initial conditions)."

**Finding that changes the text of §6 (and corrects review §5 item 6).** For Kolmogorov flow
at the native size a round-off perturbation moves a single model run's D_KY by up to 15%
(IC 2: 26.04 in float32, 21.99 in float64). The model's per-IC scatter at 1× is therefore
finite-time sampling noise of the model's own estimate. The record's reading that "the models
track the true IC dependence but amplify it" is not supported and must not appear in the paper;
per-IC scoring at 1× should be described as reducing truth-side noise only.

## 2026-10-08 — A10: truth-free selection of the main-text α = 0.1 model — PRE-REGISTRATION

Written before any of the quantities below were computed. Motivation: `k_xs01_v5` was chosen
for its 3× error against a single 40-unit truth after seven variants were scored against
truths (review objection 2). The choice must not use any Lyapunov truth.

**Candidates.** k_xs01 (v1), v2, v3, v4, v5, v7. v6a is excluded: its training was bit-identical
to v5 (inert w_rank), so it is the same model.

**Why not the stored validation loss.** `best_val_kmax_mse` is measured at each model's own
k_max (12–32) and in each model's own latent coordinates (autoencoders with different
regularizers), so the numbers are not comparable across variants.

**Step 1 — eligibility (data-only, no truth).** For each candidate, free rollouts of 1000
steps (200 time units) from IC seeds 0 and 1 on the 1×, 2× and 3× boxes; statistics over steps
100–1000. Reference: the α = 0.1 training data (wide 32π × 2π domain, 4 training trajectories,
every 10th snapshot). Two scale-local statistics, chosen because A9 showed large-scale
statistics depend on domain size: (i) vorticity std, (ii) kinetic-energy share in the forcing
band |k| ∈ [3, 5]. A candidate is eligible if, averaged over the two ICs, both statistics are
within 15% of the training-data value at every size.

**Step 2 — selection (data-only, no truth).** Among eligible candidates, pick the lowest
normalized physical-space forecast error on the held-out validation trajectory: the model is
deployed on the full periodic 32π × 2π validation domain (128 × 8 sites), encodes the
standardized field at start times t = 0, 10, 20, …, 180 (snapshots), rolls freely for k steps,
decodes, and is scored by MSE / Var(u) against the validation data at t + k. The selection
metric is the mean over start times at k = 8 (1.6 time units, ≈ 0.13 Lyapunov times at
λ₁ ≈ 0.08: short enough that Lemma 3's contraction reward is negligible). k = 1 and k = 16
are reported but not used.

**Ties.** If two eligible candidates differ by < 2% in the selection metric, prefer the one
with the smaller maximum statistic deviation in step 1.

**If no candidate is eligible,** select by step 2 alone and report the failed eligibility.

**What follows from the choice.** The selected model becomes the main-text α = 0.1 model
(Table IV, Fig. 6, §6). Its truth errors are reported whatever they are. The other variants'
errors go to §8.4 / App. D. No re-selection after seeing truth errors.
